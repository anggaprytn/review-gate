# Code Of Conduct

ReviewGate follows a simple standard for project participation:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus technical discussion on facts, tradeoffs, and reproducible behavior.
- Do not harass, threaten, or target people.
- Do not share private code, tokens, private GitLab URLs, or confidential review data.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or participants that violate these expectations.
